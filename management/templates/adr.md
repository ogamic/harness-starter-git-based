# ADR template

Copy into `decisions/NNNN-slug.md`. Derive the next number from the folder. See [decisions/README.md](../decisions/README.md) for the numbering and supersede rules.

```markdown
# ADR NNNN: <title>

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by <ADR-NNNN>
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Owner:** <the Owner>

## Context
What situation forced this decision. What's non-obvious. What constraints matter.

## Decision
What we're doing. One or two sentences, no hedging.

## Alternatives considered
- **<alt>:** why not

## Consequences
What becomes easier. What becomes harder. What we'll need to watch.
```
